Abstrakt Deck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Abstrakt Deck is an audio visualizer made by ONOJK123 Art (contact: onojk123@gmail.com).

The short version: Abstrakt Deck does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data. Everything it does happens on your own computer.

Audio

  • Depending on the source you pick in Audio > Source, Abstrakt Deck listens to the sound your computer is playing (system audio, the default), to your microphone, or to an audio file you open.
  • That audio is analysed in memory, in real time, to drive the visuals (loudness, frequency bands, beats and tempo). It is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Analysis results are discarded as the visuals update.
  • Microphone access is used only while “Mic” is the selected source. You can turn it off at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

Files on your computer

  • Recordings you make with F12 are saved as video files (picture only, no sound) in your Videos\abstrakt-deck folder.
  • Videos you export are saved where you choose. They contain the audio file you selected for the export.
  • Presets and settings are saved in your user profile (%APPDATA%\abstrakt-deck on Windows).
  • These files stay on your computer. You can delete them at any time; uninstalling the app does not delete your recordings or exports.

Network

  • Abstrakt Deck does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or telemetry.

Third parties

  • Abstrakt Deck includes FFmpeg, which it runs locally to encode videos. FFmpeg is given only local files and does not send data anywhere.
  • If you installed Abstrakt Deck from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about the purchase and installation under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

Children

  • Abstrakt Deck does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date.
